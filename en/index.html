Phenoma reads the ROMs of laserdisc arcade games from 1983–84 and writes down what is in them. The pages exist — so far only in Italian.

Rather than leave this address empty, here is what is already written. Every page carries a “come si legge nei byte” section at the bottom — addresses, tables and code fragments — which needs very little Italian, and is the part you would want if you intend to check our work.
